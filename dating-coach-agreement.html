This Coaching Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between [Marina’s Legal Name / LLC Name] (“Coach”), a [State] limited liability company, and the individual identified at the time of payment (“Client”). By completing payment for the Clarity Session, Client agrees to the terms below.

1. Services

The Coach will provide one (1) Clarity Session, consisting of:

  1. A 60-minute one-on-one video call (via Zoom) at a mutually scheduled time.
  2. A written Partner Blueprint document, delivered to Client via email within forty-eight (48) business hours after the call concludes. Business hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Eastern Time, excluding US federal holidays.
  3. Up to one (1) follow-up email exchange within seven (7) calendar days after the call to clarify points from the Partner Blueprint.

The Clarity Session is a single, one-time engagement. Ongoing coaching, additional sessions, or extended support are not included and require a separate agreement.

2. Fees and Payment

  1. The fee for the Clarity Session is $497.00 USD, payable in full at the time of booking via the Coach’s designated payment processor (Stripe).
  2. Booking is confirmed only when payment has been received in full.
  3. Client is responsible for any third-party processing fees not absorbed by the Coach.

3. Cancellation, Rescheduling, and Refunds

  1. Client may reschedule once at no charge with at least forty-eight (48) hours’ notice prior to the scheduled call. Rescheduling requests received with less than 48 hours’ notice may result in forfeiture of the session at the Coach’s discretion.
  2. Refund policy: Client may request a full refund within seven (7) calendar days of payment, provided the call has not yet taken place. After the call has occurred, no refunds will be issued. The session and the Partner Blueprint are deemed delivered upon completion of the call.
  3. If the Coach cancels or is unable to deliver the Clarity Session, Client will receive either a full refund or a rescheduled session at Client’s choice.

4. Nature of Services — Important

This is coaching, NOT therapy, counseling, or mental health treatment. The Coach is not a licensed therapist, psychologist, psychiatrist, or other licensed mental health professional. The services provided are educational and developmental in nature, focused on dating, relationship readiness, and partner-selection. Nothing the Coach says or writes constitutes medical, psychological, psychiatric, or therapeutic advice.

If Client experiences depression, anxiety disorders, post-traumatic stress, suicidal thoughts, abuse-related issues, substance use disorders, or any other clinical mental health condition — whether disclosed during the Clarity Session or otherwise — the Coach will refer Client to a licensed mental health professional and may decline to continue services until appropriate clinical support is in place. Client agrees to seek clinical care from licensed providers for such matters and not to rely on the Coach for clinical treatment.

5. No Guarantee of Outcomes

Coaching outcomes depend significantly on Client’s own actions, choices, and circumstances. The Coach makes no warranty, representation, or guarantee that Client will: (a) meet a romantic partner, (b) form a relationship, (c) marry, (d) achieve any specific dating-related milestone, or (e) experience any particular emotional outcome within any specific time frame. Client acknowledges that the Coach’s role is to support Client’s own clarity, decision-making, and self-development, not to produce or guarantee results.

6. Confidentiality

  1. The Coach will treat all information Client shares during the Clarity Session and in related communications as confidential, and will not disclose such information to third parties without Client’s express written consent.
  2. Exceptions: the Coach may disclose information (a) as required by law, (b) in response to a valid subpoena or court order, (c) where the Coach reasonably believes disclosure is necessary to prevent serious harm to Client or another person, or (d) in anonymized form as part of educational content, marketing, or training, with personally identifying details removed.
  3. Client may share information from the session at Client’s discretion. The Coach asks (but does not require) that Client also use discretion regarding personal observations the Coach shares about Client’s situation.

7. Client Responsibilities

  1. Client agrees to attend the scheduled call on time and prepared, including completion of any pre-session intake form.
  2. Client agrees to provide honest information about Client’s situation, history, and goals to enable effective coaching.
  3. Client takes full responsibility for decisions made and actions taken during and after the engagement, including all dating, relationship, and personal decisions.

8. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the Coach’s total liability under this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, shall not exceed the total amount paid by Client for the Clarity Session ($497 USD). The Coach shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, including but not limited to lost opportunities, emotional distress, or relationship outcomes.

9. Intellectual Property

The Partner Blueprint and any other materials prepared by the Coach for Client are provided for Client’s personal use only. The Coach retains all intellectual-property rights, including any frameworks, methodologies, written content, and trade names referenced in the materials. Client may not republish, redistribute, sell, or commercially exploit the materials without the Coach’s prior written consent.

10. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time before the Clarity Session has occurred, subject to the refund policy in Section 3. After completion of the Clarity Session and delivery of the Partner Blueprint, this Agreement’s active obligations are concluded; provisions regarding confidentiality, intellectual property, and limitation of liability survive termination indefinitely.

11. Entire Agreement; Modifications

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties regarding the Clarity Session and supersedes any prior or contemporaneous communications. Modifications to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both parties to be effective.

12. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [Marina’s State of Residence], without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved in a court of competent jurisdiction in [County, State].

13. Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be unenforceable or invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

14. Acceptance

By completing payment for the Clarity Session via the Coach’s designated payment processor, Client acknowledges that Client has read, understood, and agreed to be bound by the terms of this Agreement.

Note: for online sales via Stripe, click-through acceptance (i.e., a checkbox at checkout that says “I have read and agree to the Coaching Services Agreement”) is generally sufficient and avoids the need for physical signatures. For higher-tier engagements (Tier 2 and above), use a real e-signature platform like DocuSign or Dubsado.
