Booking Confirmation Email

The auto-reply that fires the moment a prospect pays $497 for the Clarity Session via Stripe. Sets expectations, links them to the calendar, sends them to the intake form. Marina’s voice throughout.

Trigger: Stripe payment received → Kit applies tag clarity-paid → this email fires within 60 seconds. The Calendly link and intake form link are inserted at runtime via Kit merge tags.

The email

M · W   CONSULTING
From: Marina <hello@mwconsulting.com>
To: {{ subscriber.email }}
Subject: You’re in. Here’s what happens next.

{{ subscriber.first_name }},

Thank you for booking the Clarity Session. Payment confirmed.

Two things to do now, in this order:

  1. Book your time. Pick a 60-minute slot that works for you here. Sessions run Mon-Fri, 9am-5pm Eastern.
    Book the call →
  2. Fill out the intake form. Seven short questions, about five minutes. I read it before the call so we don’t spend our hour catching me up — we use the hour for the actual work.
    Fill out the intake →

Here’s what to expect:

  • We meet on Zoom at the time you booked.
  • 60 minutes, just us.
  • You’ll talk. I’ll ask. I’ll observe.
  • By the end you’ll know the pattern that’s costing you, the one shift to focus on next, and the action plan for the next 30 days.

Within 48 business hours after our call, you’ll receive your written Partner Blueprint — your goal, the diagnosis, your action plan. Yours to keep, return to, and use as the map for the next month.

If something comes up before the call — a question, a thought, a worry — reply to this email. I read every one personally.

— Marina

What to customize before going live

  1. Replace {{ calendly_link }} with Marina’s actual Calendly URL (set up a 60-min Clarity Session event type).
  2. Replace {{ intake_form_link }} with the Tally form URL from the intake-form spec.
  3. Replace hello@mwconsulting.com with the actual sending email (set this up in Google Workspace once domain is bought).
  4. In Kit, save this as a template. The merge tags {{ subscriber.first_name }} and {{ subscriber.email }} auto-fill per recipient.
