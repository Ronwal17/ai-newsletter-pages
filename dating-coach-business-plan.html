High-Ticket Dating Coach

Business plan · Premium 1:1 coaching for high-income professionals who want to find a partner

Core positioning: You are not a matchmaker and not a pickup coach. You coach successful people on the inner game — self-confidence, clarity, emotional readiness and partner-selection — so they can attract and choose a long-term partner on their own terms.

1. Business concept

Promise

“In 90 days, gain the confidence, clarity, and dating strategy to meet the kind of partner you actually want — without wasting another year on the wrong people.”

Why this niche works

  • High-income professionals (35-55) are time-poor, emotionally cautious, and under-served by mass-market dating apps.
  • They already pay premium for coaches in business, fitness, and finance — relationships are the missing pillar.
  • The pain (loneliness, repeated failed dating, fear of settling) is acute and recurring, which justifies high-ticket pricing.

What you do not do

  • You do not find dates or introduce people (that's a matchmaker — different license/liability).
  • You do not teach tactics to “get laid” (that's a different market and brand).
  • You do not practice therapy — you coach. Refer clinical issues to licensed therapists.

3. Service offerings (AI-assisted, tiered)

The funnel is a survey → free AI-generated answers → paid tiers. The AI lives only at the free level to demonstrate your thinking. Every paid dollar buys time with you, the human coach. All response windows are within business hours: Mon-Fri, 9am-5pm Eastern Time.

Tier 0 — Free AI taste

Free

Take the 2-minute quiz. Get 3 personalized answers in your voice, generated by AI trained on your knowledge base. Builds email list + proves your thinking.

One 60-min 1:1 Zoom with you. Written “Partner Blueprint” delivered within 48 business hours. No subscription. 100% credit toward Tier 2 if they upgrade within 14 days.

4 weekly 60-min 1:1 sessions + WhatsApp between sessions (you reply within 24 business hours, Mon-Fri 9-5 ET). Short commitment, real traction, natural bridge to the flagship.

Tier 3 — 90-Day Transformation

$7,500 - $12,000

Flagship outcome-based program. Weekly 1:1 + async access (24h business-hour reply) + partner blueprint + date debriefs. Cap: 10 active clients.

Tier 4 — VIP Retainer

$25,000 - $40,000 / yr

Executive tier. Biweekly 1:1 + priority business-hour access + annual strategy. Cap: 5 clients.

Why this ladder works: the free AI does your marketing (saves, shares, screenshots). $497 Clarity Session is the paid entry — serious enough to filter tire-kickers, small enough to say yes to after the free answers impress them. Everything else is an upgrade for people who loved the first session. No cheap subscriptions, no group calls, no brand dilution. All communication stays inside Mon-Fri 9-5 ET.

4. Methodology (your IP)

Package your coaching into a named framework clients can point to. Example — the 4-P Method:

  1. Presence — self-confidence, nervous-system regulation, inner state before dating.
  2. Picture — clarity on the partner and relationship they actually want.
  3. Presentation — profile, photos, conversation, first-date skills.
  4. Progression — evaluating fit, escalating intentionally, avoiding sunk-cost relationships.

5. Pricing strategy

  • Never show prices on the public site — price is revealed on a call. This filters serious buyers.
  • Anchor with the 12-month retainer, sell the 90-day as the “starter” (middle option gets chosen most).
  • Raise prices every 10 clients. High-ticket buyers distrust cheap coaches.
  • Offer payment plans (3 / 6 installments) but never discount the total.

6. Brand & positioning

  • Name: use your real name + tagline. Executives buy from a person, not a logo.
  • Tagline example: “Confidence and clarity for the relationship you actually want.”
  • Tone: calm, direct, grown-up. Not hype. Not “alpha” culture. Not therapy-soft.
  • Proof: collect 1-2 paragraph written testimonials and short video testimonials from day one (with permission; use initials if needed).

7. Lead generation & sales funnel (no video, carousel + AI quiz)

The funnel, end to end

  1. Catch-word carousel on Instagram / TikTok / Facebook. A 7-10 slide carousel built around one punchy promise: “Take the 2-minute quiz — get personalized answers to the 50 dating questions our clients ask most.”
  2. Link in bio points to a quiz (Typeform / Tally).
  3. Quiz / survey — 6-8 questions: age, gender, relationship goal, current blocker, biggest frustration, timeline, email.
  4. AI auto-reply — based on their answers, an LLM (Claude or GPT) picks the 3-5 most relevant questions out of your curated 50 and generates personalized answers in your voice. Sent by email within 60 seconds.
  5. Second email (24h later) — “Did that help? Ask me anything. Reply here and I’ll answer personally.” Real replies come from you.
  6. Third email (72h later) — soft pitch for Tier 1 ($29/mo AI Advisor). Low-commitment yes.
  7. Upsell loop — inside Tier 1, every time the AI hits a limit (“this needs a human”), offer Tier 2 or 3.

The “50 questions” engine

  • You write the 50 canonical questions your ICP asks (split by segment: women 30-40, men 40-55, divorced, never-married, etc.).
  • For each, you write a 2-3 paragraph “gold-standard” answer in your own voice. This is your IP.
  • An AI prompt takes the user’s quiz answers + the question bank and produces a personalized response that cites and remixes your gold-standard answers — it never invents advice from scratch.
  • This protects quality, keeps your brand voice, and means every user feels personally seen even though it’s automated.

11. Legal & ethical guardrails

  • Form an LLC (or local equivalent). Keep business and personal finances separate.
  • Use a written coaching agreement: scope, refund policy, confidentiality, not-therapy disclaimer.
  • Carry professional liability insurance for coaches ($300-600/year).
  • Have a written referral list of licensed therapists for clinical issues (depression, trauma, abuse).
  • Certification is optional but helpful for credibility: ICF, Relationship Coaching Institute, or similar.
