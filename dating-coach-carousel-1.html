First Carousel: “Successful. Single. Stuck.”

8 slides, Instagram portrait format (1080 × 1350 px). Repurpose to TikTok Photo Mode and Facebook.

Goal of this carousel: drive quiz completions. Every slide builds toward the final CTA: “Take the quiz — get 3 personalized answers.”

Visual direction

  • Palette: cream background (#f5f1ea), deep navy text (#1a1a2e), teal accent (#16a085).
  • Typography: one bold display font (e.g. Playfair Display or Fraunces) for headlines, clean sans (e.g. Inter) for body.
  • Feel: calm, grown-up, editorial. Not hype. No emojis on the slides themselves.
  • Format: 1080 × 1350 portrait in Canva. Same template across all 8 slides.
