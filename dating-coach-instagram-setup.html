Instagram Setup Pack — MW Consulting

Everything Marina needs to launch the Instagram account in 15 minutes. Username options, bio variants, profile setup, story highlights, the first 9 posts in order, and the rules for the first 30 days. Open this on her phone — copy/paste straight into Instagram.

Marina does this part on her phone (Instagram requires a real person on a real device to create the account). The whole setup, including grabbing the handle, takes about 15 minutes. Use the bio + name + first-post copy from this document.

1. Username — pick one (in this order of preference)

Try these in order. Take the first available:

  1. @mwconsulting — cleanest. Probably taken; try anyway.
  2. @mwconsulting.co — the consulting variant.
  3. @mw.consulting — with the period separator.
  4. @marinamw — if Marina’s last initial is W, this works.
  5. @marina.mw — same with separator.
  6. @mwconsultingmiami — geographic anchor.
  7. @mw_consulting — underscore variant (last resort — underscores feel dated).
Avoid: anything with “dating,” “coach,” or “love” in the handle. Keeps the brand premium and lets MW Consulting expand into other verticals later without a rename.

2. Display name (the bold name above the bio)

Different from the @ handle. This is what shows in big text on the profile. Instagram allows up to 30 characters and indexes this for search.

4. Profile photo

For now: use Marina’s portrait (the marina-at-sunset photo). Upload from her phone’s photo library — Instagram crops to a circle automatically. The dark background and warm light make her recognizable even at thumbnail size.

Later: once the brand assets are uploaded everywhere, swap to the MW monogram on deep green for consistency with the website. But the human face converts better in early stages, so start with the portrait.

6. Switch to a Business profile

This is critical — without it, you can’t see analytics, run ads, or connect to Facebook for cross-posting.

1
Open Instagram → profile (bottom right) → menu (top right, three lines) → Settings and privacy
2
Tap Account type and tools → Switch to professional account
3
Pick category: Personal Coach (closest fit). Skip if it tries to make you display the category publicly.
4
Choose Business (NOT Creator). Business gives you Meta Business Suite access for cross-posting to Facebook + analytics + later, ads.
5
When prompted, connect to Facebook. Create a new Facebook Business Page for “MW Consulting” if Marina doesn’t have one. (5 minutes.) This unlocks cross-posting to Facebook from one place.
6
Add contact info: business email hello@mwconsulting.com (once the domain is set up). Skip phone for privacy.

9. Posting cadence (after the first 9)

  • 3 posts per week — Mon, Wed, Fri morning (8-10am ET, when the ICP is online)
  • 2 stories per day — informal, behind-the-scenes, polls, “ask me anything”
  • 1 carousel per week — rotate through the 4 carousels we built, then start designing new ones from the knowledge base
  • 2 quote tiles per week — pull lines from Marina’s polished answers in the knowledge base
  • 1 personal post per week — her face, her life, her reflections

10. Engagement rules for the first 30 days

  1. Reply to every comment within 1 hour of posting. The first hour is the algorithm window.
  2. Reply to every DM within 24 hours. Even “hi.” Especially “hi.”
  3. Spend 15 minutes a day commenting on the posts of accounts in the same niche or adjacent (relationship therapists, executive coaches, women-in-leadership accounts). Substantive comments only — no “love this!”
  4. Use the “Comment READY” CTA on every carousel. Reply to each one with the quiz link via DM, manually for the first 50. After that, automate via ManyChat.
  5. Do NOT buy followers, do NOT use bots, do NOT engagement-pod. Premium brands die from these.
