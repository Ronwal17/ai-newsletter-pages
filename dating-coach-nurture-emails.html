I want to tell you about someone I worked with last year. I’ll call her J.

J is 44. Successful, divorced, two kids, the life that looks complete from the outside. She came to me because she kept ending up with the same kind of man — charming, distant, comes back when she pulls away, leaves when she leans in. She’d done it three times since the divorce.

The work wasn’t about meeting different men. It was about understanding why her body had learned to read “uncertainty” as “love.” That’s a different thing.

Six months later, J was dating someone she would have completely overlooked a year before. He’s steady. He shows up the same way every Tuesday and every Saturday. The first weeks she said it felt “boring.” Then she realized boring was just calm in a body that had never trusted calm before.

They’re still together. She told me last month: “I didn’t know it was supposed to feel like this.”

The work itself isn’t complicated. It’s seeing the pattern, naming it, and slowly rewiring what your body trusts. That’s it.

If your version of J is the loop you keep ending up in — reply and tell me. I’ll show you what the first move would look like.

What to customize before going live

  1. Replace [Stripe payment link] in Emails 4 and 5 with the actual Stripe Payment Link Marina creates.
  2. Email 3’s “J” story is fictional but plausible. Once Marina has a real client testimonial (anonymized), swap it in. Realer beats invented every time.
