The 50 Canonical Dating Questions

Knowledge base for the AI answer engine. Every AI reply must be grounded in your written answers below.

How to use this doc: these 50 questions cover ~95% of what high-income dating clients ask. You write a 2-3 paragraph answer in your voice for each one. The AI never invents advice — it only selects, personalizes, and blends your answers based on the user’s quiz responses.

Category 1 — Self-confidence & mindset 10 Q

  1. I’m successful in every other area of my life — why do I feel so insecure in dating?
  2. How do I stop caring so much about what the other person thinks of me?
  3. My last relationship ended badly. How do I date again without being bitter or guarded?
  4. I feel like I’m “too much” for most people. How do I stop shrinking myself?
  5. How do I stop feeling like I’m running out of time?
  6. I’ve been told I’m intimidating. Is that on me or on them?
  7. How do I deal with the fear of being alone forever?
  8. What’s the difference between high standards and unrealistic expectations?
  9. I keep comparing myself to my friends who are married. How do I stop?
  10. How much of my dating struggle is actually a confidence problem?

Category 2 — Attracting the right partner 10 Q

  1. How do I figure out what kind of partner I actually want (not what I should want)?
  2. Why do I keep attracting the same wrong type of person?
  3. Are “opposites attract” relationships real, or a trap?
  4. How important is physical attraction compared to everything else?
  5. How do I know the difference between a type and a pattern I should break?
  6. Do high-achieving people need to date other high-achievers?
  7. How do I attract someone emotionally available instead of the usual distant types?
  8. Is chemistry something you can build, or is it there from day one?
  9. What are the real green flags — beyond “he has a job” and “she’s kind”?
  10. How do I stop chasing potential and start dating who’s actually in front of me?

Category 3 — Dating apps & profile 10 Q

  1. Which dating app is actually worth my time if I’m looking for a serious partner?
  2. How do I write a profile that attracts quality and repels time-wasters?
  3. What photos should I use — and which ones are quietly hurting me?
  4. How many matches should I be talking to at once?
  5. Should I mention my job/income/success on my profile, or play it down?
  6. How do I handle the match-to-date conversion problem (lots of chat, no dates)?
  7. Is paid matchmaking ever worth it over apps?
  8. How long should I chat before suggesting a meet-up?
  9. What’s the right opener — and what kills a conversation instantly?
  10. Should I delete the apps for a while? When is a break helpful vs avoidance?

Category 4 — Early dating & first dates 10 Q

  1. What’s the ideal first-date format for something serious?
  2. How do I tell the difference between nerves and a real “no” after a first date?
  3. Who should pay, and does it matter?
  4. What questions actually reveal compatibility on a first or second date?
  5. How do I avoid the over-sharing trap on early dates?
  6. When and how should I talk about past relationships or divorce?
  7. Is three dates enough to know if there’s something real?
  8. Should I sleep with them early, or wait? What’s the real tradeoff?
  9. What do I do when I’m attracted to someone who isn’t my usual “type”?
  10. How do I end things early without feeling cruel?

Category 5 — Relationship readiness & progression 10 Q

  1. How do I know if I’m actually ready for a relationship, or just lonely?
  2. When should we have “the talk” about exclusivity?
  3. How soon is too soon to talk about kids, marriage, or living together?
  4. What are the real dealbreakers vs the ones I should let go of?
  5. How do I stop running away the moment things get serious?
  6. How do I bring up hard topics (money, exes, family) without killing the vibe?
  7. What’s a healthy pace for a relationship between two busy people?
  8. How do I protect my independence while building a partnership?
  9. What does “emotional availability” actually look like day to day?
  10. How do I tell if it’s the right person, or just a familiar feeling?
