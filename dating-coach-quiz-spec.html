Field-by-field spec

FIELD 1

How old are you?

Tally type: Multiple choice (single select)

Required: Yes

Field key (for webhook): age_range

Options:
  • Under 30
  • 30–39
  • 40–49
  • 50–59
  • 60+

Why a range, not a number: faster to answer, doesn’t feel invasive, gives the AI enough context to calibrate tone.

FIELD 2

How do you identify?

Tally type: Multiple choice (single select)

Required: Yes

Field key: gender

Options:
  • Woman
  • Man
  • Non-binary
  • Prefer not to say

Used by AI to choose pronouns and avoid gendered missteps. Marina’s voice rules forbid gendered generalizations regardless.

FIELD 3

What are you actually looking for right now?

Tally type: Multiple choice (single select)

Required: Yes

Field key: goal

Options:
  • A long-term partner / marriage
  • Something serious, not sure how serious yet
  • I’m getting back into dating after a long pause
  • I’m not sure what I want

If they pick “not sure” or “getting back into dating,” the AI prioritizes mindset / readiness questions. If “long-term partner,” it prioritizes selection / progression.

FIELD 4

What’s the biggest thing getting in your way right now?

Tally type: Long text (textarea)

Required: Yes

Min length: 30 characters

Placeholder: “Be honest. The more specific, the better the answers I send back. (e.g. I keep falling for the same wrong type, I freeze on dates, I don’t know where to meet people…)”

Field key: blocker

This is the most important field. The AI uses this free-text answer to match the 3 most relevant questions from the knowledge base. The 30-char minimum forces a real answer instead of “idk.”

FIELD 5

What feels heaviest about dating right now?

Tally type: Long text (textarea)

Required: Yes

Min length: 20 characters

Placeholder: “The fear, the frustration, the loneliness, the time pressure — whichever is loudest right now.”

Field key: frustration

Captures emotional state for the AI to address tone. A user feeling “running out of time” gets a different response than one feeling “pure exhaustion.”

FIELD 6

Where should I send your answers?

Tally type: Email

Required: Yes

Field key: email

Subtext below the field: “Just your email — no spam, no list-bombing. You’ll get the personalized answers + occasional emails from me when I have something worth saying.”

Make.com scenario (the connecting glue)

One scenario, four steps. Build once, runs forever.

#ModuleWhat it does
1Tally → Watch Form SubmissionsTriggers on every quiz submit. Receives the JSON above.
2Anthropic → Make a MessageSends the system prompt + the user’s answers + the knowledge base. Receives 3 personalized answers back from Claude.
3ConvertKit → Add Subscriber + Send EmailTags the user (segment, blocker, goal). Inserts Claude’s output into the email template. Sends from hello@datingwithmarina.com.
4ConvertKit → Apply Tag “quiz-day-0”Triggers the 5-email nurture sequence (sent over the next 7 days).

Build checklist

  1. Sign up for Tally.so (free).
  2. Create the form using the spec above. Copy each field exactly.
  3. Set the form URL slug to dating-diagnosis.
  4. Style the form with brand colors: cream background, navy text, teal accent.
  5. Don’t add the webhook yet — first build the Make.com scenario, then paste the webhook URL into Tally.
  6. Test the form yourself. Submit it 3 times with different fake answers. Confirm it triggers the AI and sends a real email.
  7. Once it works end-to-end, add the form link to Stan Store / Beacons / link-in-bio.

Time to build: 30 minutes for the form, ~3 hours for the Make.com scenario, ~30 minutes for testing. One afternoon, total.
