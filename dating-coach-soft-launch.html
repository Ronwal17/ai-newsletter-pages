Soft Launch Script

Marina’s playbook for getting the first 20 real people through the quiz. Goal: 15 quiz completions, 3-5 honest replies, 1 conversation that turns into a Clarity Session within 30 days.

Part 1 — The first carousel post

Post Carousel #1 (“Successful. Single. Stuck.”) on Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook simultaneously. Use this caption:

CAPTION

Soft-launching something I’ve been quietly building.

If you’re successful in every other area of life and dating just won’t click — it’s not random. It’s usually one of four patterns, and most people can’t see which one is theirs from the inside.

I built a 2-minute quiz that figures out which one is costing you the most — and sends 3 personalized answers to your inbox in under 60 seconds. Free. No call, no pitch, no pop-ups.

If you have a friend in their 30s, 40s, or 50s who’s tired of guessing — send them this. The quiz is calibrated for them.

Link in bio — or comment READY and I’ll send it to you.

#datingcoach #relationshipcoach #datingoverforty #selfworth #confidence #readyforlove #datingadvice #singleandsuccessful

Posting tips

  • Time: 8–10 AM ET on a Tuesday or Wednesday. Highest engagement for this audience.
  • Format: upload all 8 carousel slides as a single Instagram post. On TikTok, use Photo Mode. On Facebook, post as a single image post (use slide 1) plus the caption above.
  • The first 60 minutes: respond to every comment within an hour. Engagement window matters more than the post itself.
  • Stories: share the post to stories twice that day with text overlay “new on my feed → take the quiz”.

Part 2 — The 20-person target list

Pick 20 people. Mix:

5 people
Who fit the ICP exactly — single, 30s/40s/50s, professional, looking for something serious. They’ll give the most useful feedback. Some may convert.
5 close friends
People who’ll give honest reactions even if it stings. For voice and clarity feedback.
5 professional
Therapists, executive coaches, wealth advisors, divorce attorneys. For referral relationships long-term.
5 wildcards
People who don’t fit any of the above — ex-colleagues, former clients in other contexts, second-degree connections. Best signal on whether the offer travels beyond your network.

Part 3 — The 3 DM/message versions

Use the right version for the right relationship. Send manually, one by one — never copy-paste 20 at once.

DM / MESSAGE

You’re someone whose feedback I trust. Could you take it and tell me honestly what lands and what doesn’t? Just looking for real reactions before I put it out wider.

Thank you — means a lot.

Marina

Part 5 — Soft follow-up (Day 5 after sending)

For anyone who hasn’t taken the quiz after 5 days, send a single soft follow-up:

FOLLOW-UP DM

Hey — no rush at all, but in case my last message got buried, here’s the link again: [URL]

Just need 2 minutes and your honest reaction. Even a “not for me, but here’s why” is gold.

One follow-up. Never two. If they don’t respond, let it go.

Part 6 — What to do with the feedback

  • If everyone says the email feels generic → tighten the personalization rule. Re-test 3 personas before continuing.
  • If everyone says the quiz is too long → cut Field 5 (frustration) and merge it into Field 4 (blocker). Test 5 more.
  • If 5+ people reply asking real questions → the funnel works. Time to turn on Meta ads.
  • If 1 person says “can I work with you?” → reply with template #3 (ready-to-work). That’s your first paying client.
